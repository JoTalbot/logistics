# Commercial Automation Batch V4 — 2026-09-10

## Scope
Advance market intelligence from provider snapshots into durable economics, explainable opportunity recommendations and operational human-review controls.

## Implemented

- Added `market_observation_history` as append-only historical provider snapshots.
- Hardened market-observation upsert so stale observations cannot overwrite newer current state.
- Added freshness checks and explicit verified-field extraction.
- Added deterministic route economics: direct cost, risk reserve, clean profit, risk-adjusted profit and margin rate.
- Added historical market-price trend bucketing and conservative recommended-price floor.
- Added durable `review_audit` records and explicit approve/reject/hold review service.
- Added authenticated review queue and metrics API.
- Added deterministic `recommend_opportunity()` combining route economics with compatible recent market evidence. Market data remains advisory and cannot lower the economic safety floor; currency mismatches are ignored rather than guessed.
- Added migration `0011_opportunity_recommendations.sql` to persist recommendation price, market median, reasons and update timestamp on canonical opportunities.
- Added `recommendation_store.py` for tenant-scoped persistence of recommendation outputs.
- Added unit coverage for recommendation persistence.
